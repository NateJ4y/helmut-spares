## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the legacy of an authentic automotive workshop established in 1975 in Vereeniging, South Africa. Grounded in decades of hands-on expertise with air-cooled VW Beetles, Beach Buggies, and modern VW/Audi platforms, the interface balances tactile workshop heritage with structured, contemporary e-commerce efficiency.

The aesthetic fuses **Utilitarian Workshop Craft** with **Modern Industrial Minimalism**:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, dependable, mechanical, and community-rooted.
- **Visual Personality:** High-contrast technical clarity, precise 1px mechanical joinery, warm newsprint/workshop paper backgrounds, and heavy industrial typography evocative of stamped metal badges and vintage technical service manuals.
- **Restraint:** Absence of glossy skeuomorphism, flashy gradients, or distracting animations. Every line, badge, and rule serves functional readability and mechanical trust.

## Colors

The palette references industrial workshop materials, grease-pencil technical drawings, and retro motorsport heritage:

- **Primary Accent (`#D9531E` - Burnt Orange):** Directs the user's eye to high-intent actions—primary CTAs, sale tags, cart interactions, and part availability flags.
- **Secondary Accent (`#1F4A43` - Petrol Green):** Grounding tone utilized for structural utility bars, header top-strips, technical specifications tables, and institutional badge backdrops.
- **Tertiary / Success (`#2E7D4F` - Deep Forest Green):** Indicates immediate stock status ("In Stock at Vereeniging Depot"), verified compatibility, and successful checkout states.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - Canvas: Warm workshop paper (`#F5F1E8`) to reduce sterile glare and add tactile warmth.
  - Surface Containers: Light cream paper (`#FAF7F2`) and pristine white (`#FFFFFF`) for product display tiles.
  - Ink Hierarchy: Dense technical black (`#17181A`) for primary readability, secondary oil-tone gray (`#5C5852`) for vehicle fitment specs, and muted stamp gray (`#87827A`) for part numbers and SKU labels.
- **Integrations:** Direct WhatsApp hotline link (`#25D366`) dedicated to fast mechanical consultation and custom part requests.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes condensed mechanical headings with highly legible, modern body copy:

- **Headings & Badges (`Barlow Condensed`):** Set with uppercase styling for all primary sections, category titles, and technical data headers. Gives the catalog a stamped, industrial parts-bin presence reminiscent of workshop manuals and stamped alloy chassis plates.
- **Body & Data Grid (`Inter`):** Never set below 14px (`body-sm`), maintaining a baseline 16px (`body-md`) with relaxed 1.6 leading for product fitment charts, delivery terms, and mechanical assembly advice.
- **Labels & Micro-Tags:** Barlow Condensed with heightened tracking (`0.05em` to `0.08em`) applied to SKU labels, engine codes (e.g., 1600cc Twin Port, AB Code), and heritage insignias.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured, disciplined 12-column grid forms the foundation of all catalog and transaction pages:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with a fixed container max-width of `1280px`, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2rem` outer page margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer safe margins and `1rem` guttering.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows strict structural multiples of 4px and 8px:
  - Tight component insets use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px).
  - Standard card interior padding utilizes `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px).
  - Vertical section margins rely on `space-xl` (40px) or `margin` (32px) to allow dense parts arrays to breathe cleanly without visual chaos.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated, blur-heavy shadows in favor of **Crisp Mechanical Precision**:

- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** The core mechanism for separation. All cards, inputs, and interactive surfaces rely on a crisp `1px solid #D8D1C2` border.
- **Tonal Layering:** 
  - Level 0 (Base Canvas): Warm workshop paper (`#F5F1E8`).
  - Level 1 (Product Cards / Modular Sections): Lightened tone (`#FAF7F2`) or crisp tinted white (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Level 2 (Selected States & Menus): Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border shift to `#17181A` or primary accent `#D9531E`.
- **Shadows:** Minimal, utilitarian, and physical:
  - **Resting:** `box-shadow: none` or subtle `0 1px 2px rgba(23, 24, 26, 0.04)`.
  - **Hover (Interactive Parts Cards / CTAs):** `0 4px 12px rgba(23, 24, 26, 0.08)`, moving slightly upward with crisp 1px borders retained.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft Mechanical Radius (`roundedness: 1`)**:

- **Corner Geometry:** Components have a subtle `4px` (`0.25rem`) radius. This softens raw corners while preserving the feel of industrial steel shelving, technical index cards, and machined components.
- **Badges & Micro-tags:** Use a maximum radius of `2px` or `4px` to maintain a rigid, stamped-tag look. Full pill radii (`9999px`) are strictly avoided except for floating floating status indicators (e.g., active WhatsApp chat triggers).
- **Rule Lines:** 1px hairline horizontal and vertical dividers to section technical specs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart, PayFast Checkout):** Solid burnt orange (`#D9531E`) background, crisp white text in `Barlow Condensed` bold uppercase (`label-lg`), `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding, subtle `4px` radius. Hover state transitions to `#C04515`.
- **Secondary Action (View Schematics, Filter Spares):** Outlined button with `1.5px solid #17181A`, transparent background, dark text `#17181A`. Hover transitions to `#17181A` background with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **WhatsApp Direct Assist:** `#25D366` background with dark or white text, paired with WhatsApp icon for real-time parts verification.

### Product & Fitment Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF7F2` backgrounds enclosed by `1px solid #D8D1C2`.
- Upper zone displays the part image on a neutral, shadow-free background.
- Lower zone features the part title in `headline-sm`, followed by bold engine fitment tags (e.g., "VW Beetle '68-'79 · 1600cc"), SKU in `code-tech`, and prominent orange price display.

### Chips & Badges
- **Heritage Badge:** Petrol green (`#1F4A43`) fill with white stamped uppercase tracking: `EST. 1975 · VEREENIGING`.
- **Availability Chips:**
  - *In Stock:* Light green tint background with `#2E7D4F` text and a solid 6px dot indicator.
  - *Out of Stock / Special Order:* Warm paper `#F5F1E8` with `#5C5852` text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields (Search by OEM / VIN / Engine Code):** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D8D1C2`, text `#17181A`, height `48px`, font `Inter 16px`. Active/focus state produces a sharp `1.5px solid #D9531E` ring with zero shadow glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square and circular selectors with `1.5px solid #5C5852` borders; active state fills with `#D9531E`.

### Trust Markers & Engineering Banners
- **Banner Layout:** Full-width `#1F4A43` strip with uppercase `Barlow Condensed` headers detailing:
  - "Nationwide Courier Delivery via The Courier Guy"
  - "Secure PayFast Checkout (Card, Instant EFT, Mobicred)"
  - "Vaal Triangle Spares Specialists Since 1975"
- Divided cleanly by subtle `#D8D1C2` vertical rules.
