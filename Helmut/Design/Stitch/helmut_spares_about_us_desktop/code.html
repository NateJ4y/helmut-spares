In 1975, the automotive landscape of the Vaal Triangle was defined by manufacturing muscle, open roads, and an undeniable affinity for air-cooled machinery. Helmut Spares opened its doors on Rhodes Avenue in Vereeniging with a focused objective: to supply precision-engineered, authentic components that mechanics could rely on without compromise.

Over nearly five decades, our workshop counter has served generations of South African drivers. We have supplied everyone from grandfather-and-grandson teams restoring their first ’67 Bug in a home garage, to commercial workshops rebuilding fleets of delivery runabouts and custom Beach Buggy enthusiasts engineering dune-ready sand rails.

Automotive trends shift, but mechanical integrity remains constant. Whether it is hard-to-source rubber window scrapers, pushrod tubes, Solex carburettor rebuild kits, clutch pressure plates, or complete crankcase hardware, our counter staff draws on genuine mechanical familiarity. We know the exact thread pitches, the clearance tolerances, and the differences between South African-assembled models and German imports.
